# Launch Kit

Positioning:

```text
Your life, work, and relationships as a local Obsidian graph that AI can actually use.
```

## Assets

- 20-second GIF: `docs/assets/digital-brain-demo.gif`
- Native Obsidian graph screenshot: `docs/assets/obsidian-graph-mode.png`
- Person memory screenshot: `docs/assets/obsidian-person-context.png`
- Conversation continuity screenshot: `docs/assets/obsidian-context.png`
- Fake-data sample vault: generate with `digital-brain showcase --out ./demo-assets`

## 20-Second Demo Flow

1. Install/init command.
2. Obsidian vault appears.
3. Native Obsidian graph shows people, sources, projects, and memory.
4. Person context shows tone, sources, and open loops.
5. End on `npx digital-brain init`.

## 60-Second LinkedIn/X Video Script

```text
I built Digital Brain.

It turns your digital footprint into a local Obsidian graph:
WhatsApp, iMessage, Slack, LinkedIn exports, Gmail, Calendar, and GitHub repos.

The point is not another chatbot.
The point is context: who people are, what you are working on, how you talk, and what open loops exist.

It is local-first, open source, and npm-installable.

npx digital-brain init
digital-brain run
```

## Reddit / HN Notes

- Lead with what it does, not hype.
- Mention local-first and privacy limitations early.
- Use fake-data screenshots only.
- Ask for integration feedback, not stars.
- Do not fake downloads, stars, comments, or installs.
